Graphic Reference v0.1

This image is NOT a specification.

It is NOT the final publication layout.

It is NOT a UI design.

It is NOT a rendering template.

It is only a visual reference demonstrating the class of graphical artifact expected from Graphic Publisher.

The renderer MUST NOT depend on this exact composition.

The following may change at any time:

logo
typography
spacing
iconography
branding
block arrangement
legends
footer
QR position
service information

Only the semantic information structure is expected to remain compatible.

The Graphic Publisher shall be capable of rendering future templates without changing the Publisher business logic.
